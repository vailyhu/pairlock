# guard_round: work while a guard walks his round

Side b does noisy or visible work step by step while a guard (a lamp, a searchlight, a camera) walks a round of stations; side a watches where he is. Working while he stands at a dangerous station gets b caught. Built for Szezonzárás (2026-11-17); generic. Shared contract: `../CLAUDE.md`.

- Two-sided: usually `sides: {a: {at: '<watch room>'}, b: {at: '<work room>'}}`. `a` (watcher) sees the stations, on `background` by `x`, `y` (shares of the picture) or as a row without one, dangerous ones marked, and the lamp on the current station, live. `b` (worker) sees the work steps as buttons and a clock (game time), never the stations or which is dangerous. Alone the player works from the clock and a schedule found in the game; `a` is not needed. Without places alone (`side: "both"`) one player sees both.
- `options`: see `types.ts`. `stations` (`{id, label, x?, y?, danger?}`), `stepSeconds` (real seconds per station, default 6), `clock: {startMinute, minutesPerStation}` (minutes since midnight, shown as HH:MM; the clock advances one `minutesPerStation` per station, so a schedule in the game can match the round), `steps`, `stepTexts`, `caughtText`, `caughtMessage` (default `GUARD CAUGHT`), `doneText`, `watchText`, `workText`, `background`, `awayText`.
- `move`: `{watch: true}` (the client sends it when the panel opens or the guard is paused: he starts walking), `{step: n}` (side b only, the next step in order; others are ignored).
- A step while the guard stands at a `danger` station: caught. Progress goes back to 0, `caughtText` to both, and the game message `(GAME: <caughtMessage>)` (not silent, not `bad`) for the world, e.g. an `onMessage` that raises an alarm counter. Otherwise the step counts and its `stepTexts` line shows to both. The last step without being caught solves (`doneText`).
- Timing is on the server: the guard's place and the clock come from the start time and the stations passed before; one panel timer pushes fresh views each time he moves on. `leave` (a player left, restart) pauses him where he stands; an open panel starts him again. Saved state: start time, stations passed, steps done, times caught.
- Test: `node tools/test_guard_round.js`.
